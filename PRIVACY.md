# Privacy policy

Quill Keyboard works entirely offline.

- **No internet access.** The app doesn't have the internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere.
- **Nothing is collected or shared.** There are no accounts, analytics, ads or crash reports.
- **What you type stays on your device.** Word suggestions and autocorrect use dictionaries that ship with the app.
  What you type, the dictionaries and the words the keyboard learns never leave your device.
- **Learned words are stored locally.** You can delete them at any time in Settings → Clear learned words. Android's
  own device backup, if you turned it on, may include them along with the rest of the app's settings.
- **Private fields are respected.** No words are learned in fields that ask for incognito input, and no suggestions
  are shown in password, e-mail and URL fields.
