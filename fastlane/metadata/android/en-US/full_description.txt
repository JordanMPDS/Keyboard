Quill Keyboard is a private, offline keyboard with word suggestions and autocorrect. It is based on Fossify Keyboard.<br><br>
<b>✍️ WORD SUGGESTIONS AND AUTOCORRECT:</b><br>
Get word suggestions while you type in English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, German and French. Optional autocorrect fixes typos when you finish a word and restores missing accents, and pressing backspace right after a correction undoes it. New words you type are learned on your device and can be cleared at any time.<br><br>
<b>📶 FULLY OFFLINE:</b><br>
Quill Keyboard has no internet permission. Everything, including the dictionaries and the words it learns, stays on your device.<br><br>
<b>🌐 MANY LANGUAGES AND LAYOUTS:</b><br>
Choose from a wide range of languages and keyboard layouts, and switch between them easily.<br><br>
<b>📋 HANDY CLIPBOARD:</b><br>
Save clips and pin the ones you use most, so you can insert them quickly.<br><br>
<b>🎨 CUSTOMIZABLE:</b><br>
Adjust the colors, the keyboard height, vibration, sounds and popups on key presses. A dark theme is used by default.<br><br>
<b>🔓 OPEN SOURCE:</b><br>
Quill Keyboard is open source under the GPL-3.0, so its code can be audited by anyone.<br><br>
