## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-28
First release of Quill Keyboard, based on Fossify Keyboard 1.9.1.

### Added
- Word suggestions while typing with English, Spanish, Portuguese, German and French layouts
- Optional autocorrect; press backspace right after a correction to undo it
- The keyboard learns new words you type; learned words can be cleared in settings

### Fixed
- Fixed the letter ё being untypeable on the Russian keyboard; long-press the е key to type it

For the changes of Fossify Keyboard up to 1.9.1, see [its changelog](https://github.com/FossifyOrg/Keyboard/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
